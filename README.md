## Hi, I'm Zay

I'm studying **Human-Centered Computing and Psychology** at Universität Duisburg-Essen.

Alongside university, I work with an existing production voice agent. My work mainly involves reviewing conversations, testing different scenarios, improving prompts and conversation flows, and helping investigate why calls do not behave as expected.

I also build my own small AI-powered applications. Most of them start with a problem I experience myself: too many job alerts, disorganized lecture material, an old codebase I no longer understand, or information that takes too much manual effort to sort.

I'm still developing my engineering foundations, especially around backend development, testing, deployment, and system design. I learn best by building something usable, finding its weaknesses, and improving it.

## What I'm currently working with

* Testing and improving voice-agent conversations

* React, TypeScript, Python and SQLite

I'm particularly interested in the space between a real-world problem and a working technical solution: understanding the workflow, deciding where AI is useful, and building a first version that can be tested.

## Selected projects

### [AI Support Panel](https://github.com/zayzyyazy/AI-Support-panel)

A support dashboard that turns incoming messages into structured tickets.

It uses AI to suggest a category, priority and escalation status, with rule-based fallback logic when needed. It can also calculate SLA deadlines and create tickets through HubSpot or a mock CRM.

`React` · `Express` · `Electron` · `OpenAI API` · `HubSpot`

---

### [Job Pipeline](https://github.com/zayzyyazy/job-pipeline)

A local tool for organizing and evaluating job alerts from Gmail.

It extracts job information from emails, scores roles against defined criteria and stores the results in SQLite. I built it because manually opening and comparing every job alert was becoming repetitive.

`Python` · `Flask` · `SQLite` · `Gmail API` · `OpenAI API`

---

### [Course Dashboard](https://github.com/zayzyyazy/course-dashboard)

A desktop study application for working with university lecture PDFs.

It extracts lecture content and creates summaries, key concepts, quizzes and topic explanations. It also includes an AI chat scoped to the currently selected lecture.

`Electron` · `React` · `TypeScript` · `Node.js` · `SQLite`

---

### [Activity Intelligence](https://github.com/zayzyyazy/activity-intelligence)

An experimental productivity app that combines captured tasks with ActivityWatch data and suggests one next action.

This project is my attempt to build something more useful than another long to-do list.

`Tauri` · `React` · `TypeScript` · `Rust` · `ActivityWatch API`

---

### [Project Explainer](https://github.com/zayzyyazy/project-explainer)

A local desktop app that scans a codebase and generates a structured explanation of what it contains.

I built it because I often returned to older projects and had to spend time understanding them again.

`Tauri` · `React` · `TypeScript` · `Rust` · `SQLite` · `Claude API`
